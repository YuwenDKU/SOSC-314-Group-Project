# SOSC-314-Group-Project：Yuwen Zhou; Tianyi Xie ; Meina
**Research Question：**
Do cities with higher fiscal capacity use more sophisticated industrial policy instruments in Eastern cities of China?
**Background**
We investigate whether a city’s fiscal capacity，measured by its general public budget revenue，
shapes its choice of policy instruments for industrial development. Specifically, we ask: do
fiscally stronger cities rely more on sophisticated instruments such as government guidance
funds and equity participation, while fiscally weaker cities default to simpler tools like land
concessions and tax reductions?
